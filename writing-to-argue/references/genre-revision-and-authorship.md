# Genre, revision, and authorship

Branch criteria for argument, explanatory, synthesis, narrative, AP Seminar, and AP Research work. Do not collapse them into one score. Revise in this order: claim and meaning; evidence and reasoning; organization; audience and style; conventions.

For AP Seminar and AP Research, mark rubric or sample version, permissions, pathway, and calibration limits. A request for final submission prose returns a scaffold, checklist, feedback, decision log, and learner-authored checkpoints. The learner chooses the final claim, evidence promotion, organization, and wording.
