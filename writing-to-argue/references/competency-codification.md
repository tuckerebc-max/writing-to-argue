# Writing to Argue competency codification

**Competency:** `COMP-WR-ARGUE`  
**Domain:** `DOM-WR` / `SKILL-WR`  
**Governing protocol:** `SPEC-COMP-001`  
**Technical specification:** `SPEC-WR-001`  
**Prompt:** `ANRI-DOM-WR-001`  
**Architecture:** `ARCH-WR-001`  
**Corpus:** `CORP-WR-001`

## Construct

Design and revise a written response that makes a defensible, proportionate claim for a defined audience and purpose; organizes reasons and evidence; makes evidence-to-claim reasoning inspectable; acknowledges alternatives and uncertainty; uses sources responsibly; and controls language and genre choices so the reader can evaluate the argument.

## Observable performances

| ID | Performance |
|---|---|
| `OP-WR-01` | Interpret the assignment and define purpose, audience, genre, and constraints. |
| `OP-WR-02` | Formulate a claim whose scope matches available evidence. |

| `OP-WR-04` | Explain commentary and warrants rather than stacking quotations or summaries. |
| `OP-WR-05` | Qualify a claim when evidence, method, or context limits certainty. |
| `OP-WR-06` | Represent and answer a strongest relevant alternative. |
| `OP-WR-07` | Organize a readable path and make provenance inspectable. |
| `OP-WR-08` | Revise substantively in response to criteria, reader questions, and evidence. |
| `OP-WR-09` | Disclose AI use and retain learner decisions, drafts, and source reading. |

## Project diagnostic dimensions

Task fit; claim scope; evidence quality/fit; commentary/warrant; alternative handling; organization; source integrity; language/control; revision quality; and authorship transparency are project implementation diagnostics unless a named course or rubric supplies an official scale.

## Source and assessment boundaries

The authority stack includes Common Core (`S003`, `S006`), SAT technical/assessment sources (`S002`, `S007/S008`), AP English Language (`S060`, `S105`), Smarter Balanced (`S101`), GRE/LSAT/ACT comparators (`S106`, `S107`, `S108`), AP Research rubric comparator (`S024`), SCONUL/IB (`S110/S112`), and bounded expansion sources (`S132`, `S134`, `S135`, `S016/S017`). These are not interchangeable rubrics. Protected AP/SAT material and student samples remain rights-bounded.

## Evidence and release conditions

The task contract and evidence record link claim IDs, reason IDs, source/evidence IDs, locators, commentary, qualifier, alternative, learner decisions, and revision history. `TST-WR-001` through `TST-WR-011`, `TST-INT-004`, and `TST-INT-005` are required. Open conditions include exact current AP locators, separation of short-form and extended writing evidence, licensed exemplars, and human review of AP/assessed authorship claims.
