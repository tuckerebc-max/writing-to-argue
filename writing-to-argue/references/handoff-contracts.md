# Writing to Argue handoffs

## From Close Reading — `HOF-CR-WR-001`

Require source IDs, evidence IDs, locators, claim status, uncertainty IDs, provenance IDs, and learner decisions. Treat a close-reading interpretation as an evidence candidate, not a source fact.

## From QUEST — `HOF-QST-WR-001`

Require bounded question, corpus boundary, source audit, perspective map, method limitations, source/evidence IDs, uncertainty, and learner decisions. Do not erase research limitations in prose.

## To Argumentation — `HOF-WR-ARG-001`

Pass task contract, main claim, reasons, evidence, commentary, qualifiers, alternatives, source IDs, evidence IDs, claim IDs, and learner decisions for warrant and burden review.

## From Argumentation — `HOF-ARG-WR-001`

Accept validated claim map, warrant audit, strongest alternative, response plan, source audit, uncertainty, and unresolved items. Do not strengthen the claim or remove a qualifier during composition.
